Trial and error is at the heart of understanding the world around us, and Reinforcement Learning (RL) is this philosophy put into effect. It's a joy to watch my agents gradually adapt and improve through their own experiences.
To this end, my latest project, DiepJAX, is a testbed for me to further explore what it truly means to create an agent that learns.
The ultimate goal for me would be to create a generalized learning agent, i.e., one that can play Doom and know how to play Halo as a result. Making J.A.R.V.I.S along the way would be a nice touch :P
Currently, I'm working on my undergraduate thesis in the field of Multi-Agent RL (the only thing better than RL) under the guidance of Prof. Stefano V. Albrecht, at NTU Singapore.

Knightmare Protocol

Jul 2025 – Feb 2026

A chess agent trained by imitation learning on 8M+ expert positions, deployed live on Lichess at about 1500 blitz Elo.

  • Implemented NFQ, DQN, Double DQN and Dueling DQN from scratch, first on a 5×5 board, then full 8×8 chess.
  • Ran systematic experiments moving from offline supervised learning to online RL.
  • Designed a canonical board representation that removes perspective asymmetry and stabilises training.
